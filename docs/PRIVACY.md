# RepToll Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

RepToll is made by Syed Safi Ali. This policy explains what the app does with your data. Short version: everything stays on your iPhone.

## Camera
RepToll uses the front camera only during a workout, to count your reps. Video is analyzed on your device by Apple's Vision framework. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

## Screen Time
RepToll uses Apple's Screen Time APIs (Family Controls) to lock the apps you choose. Apple gives the app opaque tokens, not the names of your apps or your browsing history. Your selection, usage thresholds and earned time are stored on your device only.

## Workout history and settings
Your workouts, streaks, earned time and settings are stored on your device. If you turn on "Record pose data" in Settings, joint positions from workouts are saved on your device. They are shared only if you choose to share them.

## Purchases
Subscriptions are processed by Apple. RepToll receives a confirmation of your subscription status from the App Store, but no payment details.

## Data collection
RepToll has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It does not collect, sell or share personal data. Deleting the app deletes its data.
